# Image provenance

The active images under `modern/` are original AI-generated illustrative demo assets created with the OpenAI built-in image generation tool. They do not depict actual inventory, and materials, stones, and prices are illustrative and must be confirmed before commercial use.

Catalog prompt set: distinct minimalist gold or silver necklaces, hoops, studs, rings, bracelets, bridal sets and pendants, photographed on warm ivory surfaces in soft natural light. Category prompts: distinct editorial arrangements of each jewellery category. Editorial prompts: jewellery worn with ivory clothing, a ring and bracelet on a hand, and layered chains on silk. Featured carousel images were generated from each matching demo product reference with a different camera angle and physical arrangement.

Each product has its own catalogue image. The same catalogue image is intentionally shared between that product's listing, detail and thumbnail. Featured carousel images, category cards and editorial placements have separate images. The supplied hero video remains in use.

The necklaces-editorial category image was generated as a new composition with the layered gold necklaces and pendant placed above clear space for a bottom label.
The bridal-editorial category image was generated with the pendant and studs positioned above open space for the bottom label.

Five original showcase images were generated using the built-in image generator from the matching demo product references, retaining each design with warm ivory product-photography styling.
